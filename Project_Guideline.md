## AskPool — Every Student Has a Voice

**AskPool is a live classroom Q&A platform that helps students ask questions without the pressure of speaking in front of the class.** It gives professors a clear view of what students find confusing and which questions matter most.

### The problem
Students often stay silent during lectures because they feel shy, worry about asking a “bad” question, or do not want to interrupt. As a result, misunderstandings go unresolved, and professors may struggle to gauge whether the class is following.

### How it works
Students join a lecture session through a QR code or session link. They submit questions anonymously to a shared question pool and upvote questions they would also like answered.

Questions are ranked by **votes first**. Earlier submissions come first when vote counts are tied. Professors can select questions to answer, mark them as answered, and review unanswered questions after class.

### Core features
- **Personal accounts:** Every signed-in account can use both Student and Professor. A user's professor history contains their created lectures; student history contains their visited lectures. Profiles, courses, histories, and lecture management remain scoped to the authenticated account and lecture ownership.
- **Anonymous questions to classmates:** Students can ask without displaying their names in the shared question pool. Professors may see authors.
- **Upvoting:** Students highlight shared concerns, helping professors identify common misunderstandings.
- **Professor dashboard:** Professors browse, filter, and select questions, with clear statuses such as open, selected, and answered.
- **Reporting and moderation:** Students can report spam or abusive questions. Reports flag content for review; they do not change who can see the author.
- **Accountability for misuse:** Questions remain linked to authenticated student accounts. Students are informed before submitting that professors may see authors.

### Hackathon prototype
The first version will include session creation, QR-code joining, question submission, upvoting, a live ranked question pool, and a professor dashboard with basic reporting and moderation. Each student can vote once per question, and submission limits help reduce spam.

### Expected impact
AskPool makes classroom participation more accessible, helps students discover that others share their questions, and lets professors use limited Q&A time to address the class’s most pressing needs.

**Pitch:** An anonymous classroom question pool where students ask, classmates vote, and professors respond to what matters most.
